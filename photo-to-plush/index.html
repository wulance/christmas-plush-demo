Custom plush dolls from photos: tips and ideas

The best photos are well lit, taken from the front, and show the face clearly. If the person has a signature item, like round glasses, a favorite hoodie or a team jersey, send a photo of that too, because small details make the plush recognizable.

Plush portraits of pets

Pets work just as well as people. Send photos of the face and of any special markings, such as a white sock or a spot over one eye, and our illustrators will include them.

Gift ideas

Customers order photo plush as wedding gifts (a pair of the couple), for a grandparent's birthday, for a new baby, or as a keepsake of a pet that has died. For the holidays, choose the Santa hat, or see the Christmas collection.
