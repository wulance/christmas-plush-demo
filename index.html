How we work

From sketch to plush in four steps

Design sketchColour illustration

Every plush starts as a drawing

Drag the slider, or use the arrow keys, to compare a designer's sketch of Momo, our studio mascot, with its colour illustration. We don't resell catalog toys: our in-house team draws every character, writes the sewing pattern and checks the sample. That's how we keep a mascot's expression right, all the way from the screen to the stitching.

  1. 1. Sketch

    We sketch the character and agree its personality, proportions and colors with you.

  2. 2. Pattern

    The sketch becomes a sewing pattern, and we test the 3D shape in calico.

  3. 3. Fabric & sample

    We choose the fabric, match the colors and sew a sample for you to approve.

  4. 4. Production & QC

    We sew, stuff, safety-test and inspect every piece. This usually takes 15–25 days after you approve the sample.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order?

Corporate custom plush starts at 50 pieces. Christmas and photo-to-plush items can be ordered one at a time.

How long does a custom plush take?

The first digital design takes 2–3 days and a physical sample takes 7–10 days. Production takes 15–25 days after you approve the sample, plus shipping.
