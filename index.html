How we work

From sketch to plush in four steps

  1. 1. Sketch

    We sketch the character and agree its personality, proportions and colors with you.

  2. 2. Pattern

    The sketch becomes a sewing pattern, and we test the 3D shape in calico.

  3. 3. Fabric & sample

    We choose the fabric, match the colors and sew a sample for you to approve.

  4. 4. Production & QC

    We sew, stuff, safety-test and inspect every piece. This usually takes 15–25 days after you approve the sample.
