Corporate plush FAQ

Can you design a mascot if we only have a logo?

Yes. Most of our mascots start from just a logo and a short description of the company. We'll ask a few questions about your culture and audience, and then propose 2–3 character directions.

Who owns the mascot design?

You do. When the order is complete, we transfer the character design rights to your company. (Placeholder: confirm this with your legal terms.)

Can you match our exact brand colors?

Yes. We dye or select fabric to match your Pantone references, and we send fabric swatches for approval on orders of 500 pieces or more.
