Burrow Mole, our original concept

Our own character, not a customer project. These are 3D concept renderings from design checkpoint v0.4 (option A). No physical sample has been made yet.

What checkpoint v0.4 shows

Face
Closed crescent eyes and a small “w” mouth in flat satin stitch, with the pile trimmed around the stitching. No hard parts.
Flower nose
A ring of rose felt petals on a corduroy snout that tilts slightly down.
Spade paws
Flat two-layer corduroy with rounded fingertips and a topstitched palm line.
Silhouette
A low pear-shaped dome with four seams meeting at the crown, on a flat felt base.
Size targets
About 24 cm tall and 30 cm wide, measured on the 3D model. Not verified on a sample.

From sketch to plush in four steps

Burrow Mole and Nib are both still at the first step. Steps 2–4 describe how any plush continues once a design is approved.

Design sketchColour illustration

Every plush starts as a drawing

Drag the slider, or use the arrow keys, to compare a designer's sketch of Momo, our studio mascot, with its colour illustration. Both are drawings, not product photos.

  1. 1. Sketch

    We sketch the character and agree its personality, proportions and colors with you.

  2. 2. Pattern

    The sketch becomes a sewing pattern, and we test the 3D shape in calico.

  3. 3. Fabric & sample

    We choose the fabric, match the colors and sew a sample for you to approve.

  4. 4. Production & QC

    We sew, stuff, safety-test and inspect every piece. This usually takes 15–25 days after you approve the sample.
