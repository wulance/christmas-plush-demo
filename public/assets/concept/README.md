# Burrow Mole v0.4 concept images (web derivatives)

These files are web-optimized copies of the approved Burrow Mole (option A) v0.4 concept renderings.

They are 3D concept renderings, not photographs of a manufactured sample. The images already carry that caption. Use them on the homepage and studio/design-capability presentation, with honest HTML captions or alt text as well.

Do not present them as finished product photos, customer work, reviews, or catalogue samples. Do not modify the source originals (they are not in this directory). Prefer the WebP variants with JPEG fallbacks, and choose a width from the srcset rather than serving the largest file to every screen.
