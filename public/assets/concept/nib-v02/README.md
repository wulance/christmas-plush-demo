# Nib the owlet v0.2 concept images (web derivatives)

Web-optimized copies of the approved Nib the owlet (concept version 0.2) 3D concept renderings,
made with `tools/make-nib-derivatives.py` from the untouched originals in `incoming/nib-v02/`.
Whole-frame resize only: no crop, retouching or sharpening, and never upscaled.

They are 3D concept renderings, not photographs of a manufactured sample. No physical sample
has been made. The images already carry that label; keep it in HTML alt text and captions too.
Dimensions (23.2 cm tall × 23.7 cm wide wing tip to wing tip × 19.0 cm deep) are model targets
pending sampling. The turnaround sheet shows them rounded.

`manifest.json` lists roles, pixel sizes, alt text and WebP/JPEG variants. Serve a width from the
`srcset` rather than the largest file. The originals are not copied into `public/` or the built site.
