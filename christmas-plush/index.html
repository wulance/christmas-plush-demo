How we work

From sketch to plush

  1. 1. Sketch

    Our designers sketch the character and agree its personality, proportions and colors with you.

  2. 2. Pattern

    The sketch becomes a sewing pattern. We test the shape in calico so the 3D form matches the drawing.

  3. 3. Fabric & sample

    We choose the fabric, match the colors and make a sample. You approve photos or a physical sample.

  4. 4. Production & QC

    We sew, stuff, safety-test and inspect every piece, then pack it as a gift. This usually takes 15–25 days after you approve the sample.

Why choose a plush advent calendar this Christmas?

Most advent calendars end up in the recycling bin on Christmas Eve. A plush Christmas tree advent calendar works differently: each morning your child takes a soft ornament from the pouch and puts it on the tree. By December 24 the tree is fully decorated, and you can pack it away and use it again next year.

Personalized Christmas gifts that last

A personalized reindeer plush with an embroidered name is a gift people keep for years. Many families add a new dated ornament to their tree each December. For a baby's first Christmas, a name embroidered on a small 20 cm reindeer makes a keepsake for the nursery.

Christmas gifts for employees and clients

Custom branded plush stays on desks long after other corporate gifts are forgotten. We design company Christmas plush in your brand colors, and we can design a mascot based on your logo.

Frequently asked questions

Are the plush toys safe for babies and toddlers?

Every design is tested to EN71 (EU/UK), ASTM F963 and CPSIA (US). We embroider eyes instead of using hard plastic parts, and we can supply test reports on request. (Placeholder: replace with your real certifications.)

How do the ornaments attach to the tree?

Each ornament has a soft hook-and-loop patch on the back. The tree has hidden patches that toddlers can use, and nothing on it is sharp.

Can I order a reindeer with my company logo?

Yes. Company orders start at 50 pieces. Use the reindeer builder with "This is for my company" ticked, then request a quote and we'll send a free digital proof.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to the US, UK, EU, Canada, Australia, Japan and more. The shipping section above shows the Christmas deadline for each country.

Can you turn a photo into a plush?

Yes. Our photo-to-plush service turns a person or pet into a cartoon plush character, and you can choose a festive Santa hat or sweater for Christmas.
